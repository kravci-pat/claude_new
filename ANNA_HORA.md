## The client

- **ANNA HORA**: silk pieces (30-momme mulberry silk), cut and sewn in a Prague atelier.
  Founded by two sisters: **Kat** (Dubai, has a corporate job) and **Barbora / Bara** (Prague, runs the atelier).
  Ships worldwide. No stock in Dubai yet; Dubai is the 2027 vision. Website: annahora.com.
  Also involved: Chris (role unclear).
- **Agency**: LVLELEVEN, which is Patrik + Diana. We plan, create, edit and post.
  Kat and Bara handle comments and DMs.
- **Audience**: Czech women, about 30 to 55, who run businesses or lead teams. Most current customers are Czech.
- **The one idea**: "made for the moments no one sees". Her own hour.
- **Language**: English first, Czech underneath on founder, Prague and story posts.
  Open question: formal "vy" or friendly "ty".
- **Their guideline rules**: natural light, no trend audio, no CTAs, text on image only for Brand posts,
  no emoji, banned words (luxury, timeless, effortless, elegant, curated...).
  Founder posts were meant to be 1 to 2 per season. We use the founders much more and Kat agreed to that.
- **Posting**: 08:00 Prague time, 3 posts a week (Tue, Thu, Sat) plus stories. Kat and Bara approve on Sunday in one WhatsApp message.

## Q4 content strategy (summary)

4 pillars, 12 posts a month (about 5 reels, 4 carousels, 3 photos):
The Hour (W, 4), The Cloth (G, 3), The Atelier (E, 3), The Sisters (B, 2).
Series: Two sisters two cities, The first piece, From one roll, Why silk,
The morning belongs to you, The sound of silk, Letters, Her hour, Prague slowly,
Help us choose, Packing an order, Ask the sisters.
Gifting: "a gift she gives herself" ("by herself, for herself").
Prague outreach, 5 a month: Ones Concept Store, Shop Up Stories, Charaktery, BackYard, NILA, Kuráž,
plus makers, yoga or pilates teachers and photographers.
Bara has a filming guide: 9:16, window light, 8 to 10 second shots, close-ups, real sound,
Friday upload to Drive, weekly voice note.
